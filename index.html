  • Guess The Rank Game


    A party game best played with a group of friends. Each round, everyone gets their own "Who is most likely to..." question and secretly ranks the group from most to least likely. Then, one by one, each player's ranking is put in the spotlight and everyone else has to guess exactly where a randomly chosen player was placed. Guessing the exact position earns you a point. The ranker scores too, based on how many people guessed correctly (with a maximum of 3).

  • Most Likely To Game


    A party game best played with a group of friends. Each round, everyone gets the same "Who is most likely to..." question and votes for who they think it describes. However, one player secretly receives a completely different question. The twist is that after voting, everyone has to figure out who got the odd one out. If you're the imposter, your goal is to blend in and avoid detection. If you're not, your goal is to sniff out who was answering a different question entirely.

  • Infinite Tic Tac Toe


    In this game, like Tic-Tac-Toe, you need to get 3 marks in a line (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally). However, you can only have 3 marks on the grid at a time. When you place your 4th mark, your 1st mark disappears, and so on. You can't place your 4th mark where your 1st mark was. After each player has placed 3 marks, one mark will turn red to show it will disappear. This continues until the game ends.
